# 🍫 Awesome Chocolate Sales Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes **chocolate sales performance** across multiple dimensions such as sales, cost, profit, boxes sold, and shipment patterns.  
The dashboard helps the business identify **top-performing salespeople, product categories, and logistics inefficiencies** while tracking growth trends.

---

## 📊 Key Objectives
1. Analyze **Sales, Profit, and Boxes Sold** across product categories and salespersons.  
2. Identify **low box shipment patterns** to optimize logistics and fulfillment.  
3. Compare **Month-over-Month (MoM)** and **Year-over-Year (YoY)** sales and profit trends.  
4. Evaluate **costs vs. profits** across regions and products.  
5. Build an **interactive Power BI dashboard** for real-time decision-making.  

---

## 🗂️ Data Sources
- **Sales Data (CSV)** – includes sales amount, boxes, cost, and profit  
- **Product Data** – category & subcategory details  
- **Salesperson Data** – salesperson-level performance  

---

## ⚙️ Tech Stack
- **Power BI Desktop** – Data cleaning, modeling, dashboard development  
- **DAX** – Custom measures for MoM/YoY comparisons, profit margins, and shipment patterns  
- **Excel** – Initial data transformation  

---

## 📈 Dashboard Highlights
- 💰 **Sales & Profit Overview**: Track revenue and profit margins over time  
- 👤 **Salesperson Performance**: Identify top and underperforming reps  
- 📦 **Boxes Shipped Analysis**: Spot low shipment patterns for logistics improvements  
- 📊 **MoM & YoY Comparisons**: Measure growth trends against historical performance  

---

## 🚀 How to Use
1. Clone/download this repository.  
2. Open the `.pbix` file in **Power BI Desktop**.  
3. Use filters (Year, Salesperson, Category) to interact with the dashboard.
