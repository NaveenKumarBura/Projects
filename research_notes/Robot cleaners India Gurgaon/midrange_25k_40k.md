# Mid-range Robot Vacuum + Mop Cleaners (Rs 25,000–40,000) on Amazon.in / Flipkart, India, Nov 2026

**Method (read first):** Unlike the premium pass, `curl` to Amazon.in search and product pages and to Flipkart search pages **worked** on 27 Nov 2026. All prices, ratings, rating counts, "bought in past month" figures and seller names below were **scraped live on 27 Nov 2026** from those pages. They are not search-engine snapshots. Caveats:
- Amazon groups ratings for some product families. The Dreame L10 Prime (B0FCXCN4S6) and D10 Plus Gen 2 (B0D3RGFSRB) both show 4.2★ (2,735). The Dreame L50 (B0H2D97D9Z) and L50 Plus (B0H2DD8Z5T) both show 4.1★ (1,043). These counts are **shared across variants**, not specific to the model.
- Flipkart also pools ratings across product families. ECOVACS N10, Y1 Pro and Deebot Mini all show 4.1★ (3,420). Mi X10 and Mi Robot Vacuum-Mop P both show 4.2★ (6,613). Treat these as brand-family ratings.
- Every Amazon.in product page I opened showed "Ships from: Amazon", which means Amazon-fulfilled, with a third-party or brand seller. I **could not determine Flipkart Assured** status per listing: the badge pattern was not attached to individual product cards in the HTML.
- Battery figures shown in "related product" widgets were the same across pages, so I discarded them. Battery is listed only where it appears in the model's own title or bullets.

## Which give the best self-empty or mop-wash dock in this band?

### Takeaway
Only two listings in the band have an **all-in-one dock** (auto-empty plus mop wash and dry): the **Dreame D20 Ultra (Rs 39,999, 395 ratings)**, the safer choice, and the **Tapo RV50 Pro Omni (Rs 39,999, hot-water wash and dual-laser avoidance, but only 24 ratings)**. For **self-empty only**, the best picks are the Dreame L50 Plus (25,000Pa, 120-day bag), Ecovacs N30 Plus (bagless) and Narwal X20 Plus (tri-laser avoidance). For **mop-wash only**, the Dreame L10 Prime is the proven choice, but its suction is weak (4,000Pa).

### Inferences
- In a dusty Gurgaon home, a mop-wash dock without auto-empty (L10 Prime) still means emptying the robot's bin by hand every day or so. A self-empty dock with a static or rotary mop (L50 Plus, N30 Plus) is usually the more practical buy under Rs 35k. Stretch to the D20 Ultra at Rs 39,999 for both.
- The ILIFE T20s Ultra is the cheapest well-reviewed self-empty LiDAR option (Rs 26,915, 2,324 ratings). Its suction and bag-life claims are inconsistent between its own title and bullets.

### Gaps
- No independent tests comparing dock reliability, mop wash quality or obstacle avoidance across these models.
- The Tapo RV50 Pro Omni has too few reviews to judge reliability.
